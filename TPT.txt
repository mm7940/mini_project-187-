Cross-border payments operate across multiple financial institutions and rely heavily on SWIFT message types such as MT103 for payment initiation and MT910 for confirmation.
Due to this distributed architecture, even minor discrepancies—like missing data or delays in message transmission—can trigger client enquiries.

Currently, enquiry resolution is often handled manually across multiple teams, leading to fragmented ownership and limited visibility.
This results in increased turnaround time and inconsistent resolution outcomes.

So the core challenge is ensuring that enquiry handling is not only accurate, but also standardized, traceable, and efficient across the lifecycle.










To address these challenges, we implemented CERS as a centralized enquiry management system built on Pega’s case management framework.

Each enquiry is treated as a case, enabling end-to-end lifecycle tracking—from initiation and validation to resolution and closure.

Pega provides key capabilities such as workflow orchestration, SLA-driven processing, and governance through a Maker–Checker model.
Additionally, the system integrates notification mechanisms to keep stakeholders informed at every critical stage.

This ensures that all enquiries are handled within a structured and controlled environment, acting as a single source of truth










The core strength of CERS lies in its intelligent processing capability driven by business rules and decisioning.

Once a case is created, the system performs validation on payment details, including both structured and unstructured message formats.

Based on predefined rules, a decision engine determines the processing path.
For low-risk scenarios, Straight Through Processing (STP) is applied, enabling automatic resolution without manual intervention.

For higher-risk or complex cases, the system routes the enquiry for manual review under a controlled workflow.

For example, we implemented threshold-based decisioning, where transactions below a defined limit—such as 500—are processed via STP, while those exceeding the threshold are routed for manual handling.

This approach ensures an optimal balance between automation efficiency and risk control









From a business perspective, CERS significantly reduces manual effort by automating low-risk scenarios, while ensuring proper controls for high-value transactions.

It improves turnaround time and consistency in resolution, leading to enhanced customer satisfaction.
Additionally, the system provides full auditability, which is critical for compliance in cross-border payment processing.

Most importantly, the solution is scalable and adaptable, making it suitable for global operations with varying regulatory and operational requirements.

Overall, CERS transforms enquiry resolution from a reactive, manual process into a structured, intelligent, and scalable system.
